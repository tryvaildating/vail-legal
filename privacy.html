2. What we collect

  • Account & verification: your @cam.ac.uk email address.
  • Profile information: year of study, subject, college, tags, and the prompt answers you write.
  • Photos: a single profile photo, if you choose to upload one.
  • Activity data: who you like, match with, block, and report; the messages you send within matches; your notification preferences.
  • Technical data: we don't currently run analytics or crash-reporting tools, so we don't collect device/usage analytics beyond what's necessary to operate the app (e.g., your session/login state).

We do not collect payment information (no payments exist yet), your precise location, or your contacts.

3. How we use it

  • To operate the core features: showing you candidates, handling likes and matches, delivering messages, and enforcing the reveal mechanic.
  • To verify you're a genuine Cambridge student via your @cam.ac.uk email before you can sign in.
  • To respond to support requests and review reports of misconduct.
  • To send you transactional emails (sign-in codes) — we don't send marketing email.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. We use the following service providers ("processors") to run the app, who can access relevant data only to the extent needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, file storage, and realtime messaging infrastructure.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — delivers our sign-in emails.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users (e.g., in response to a genuine safety report or legal request).

5. How your photo and identity stay hidden

Your photo is never shown to anyone — including a match — until both of you have exchanged a set number of messages and chosen to reveal it. This is enforced at the database level (not just hidden by the app's screens), so it can't be bypassed by inspecting network traffic or the app itself.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (most of this you can already edit yourself in-app, under Profile).
  • Delete your data — available immediately and directly in-app via Profile → Privacy & Safety → Delete my account, which permanently removes your profile, photos, prompts, matches, and messages.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, or ask us a question about how your data is used — contact support@vaildating.com.
  • Complain to a regulator — the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk, if you believe we've mishandled your data.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, photos, prompts, matches, and messages are permanently removed. Some minimal records (e.g., reports you've filed or that were filed against you) may be retained longer where necessary for safety and moderation purposes.

8. Blocking and reporting

If you block someone, we don't tell them they've been blocked, and it only affects what's shown in your Discover feed going forward — it doesn't retroactively hide you from a match or chat you already had with that person. If you report someone, that report is only visible to us, not the person you reported.

9. Children's privacy

Vail is intended for verified Cambridge students aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you in-app before they take effect.
